Hadith - English Translation
Collection: Forty Hadith of Shah Waliullah Dehlawi
============================================================

Book 1: Forty Hadith of Shah Waliullah Dehlawi
----------------------------------------
[1:1] The report is not like witnessing
[1:2] War is deception
[1:3] A Muslim is a mirror of the Muslim
[1:4] Someone whose advice is sought is in a position of trust
[1:5] The person guiding (someone) to do a good deed, is like the one performing the good deed
[1:6] Seek help for any needs discreetly
[1:7] Protect yourself from the fire even with a part of a date
[1:8] The world is the believer’s prison and the disbeliever’s paradise
[1:9] Modesty is entirely good
[1:10] The word of the believer is like seizing of the hand
[1:11] It is unlawful for a believer to sever relations with his brother for more than three days
[1:12] He is not one of us who cheats us
[1:13] A little that suffices is better than an abundance that distracts
[1:14] Someone who takes back his gift is like someone who eats his vomit
[1:15] Affliction comes from (loose) talk
[1:16] People are like the teeth of a comb
[1:17] Richness lies in the richness of the soul
[1:18] The felicitous person takes lessons from (the actions of) others
[1:19] Indeed, in poetry there is wisdom and in eloquence there is magic
[1:20] The king’s pardon preserves the kingdom
[1:21] A man will be with whom he loves
[1:22] A man who knows his worth will not be ruined
[1:23] The child belongs to the bed and stoning is for the adulterer
[1:24] The upper hand is superior to the lower hand
[1:25] He does not thank Allah who does not thank people
[1:26] Your love of something can blind and deafen (you)
[1:27] Hearts are predisposed to love someone who does them good and detest someone who does them harm
[1:28] One who repents from sin is like someone without sin
[1:29] The person who actually witnesses an (event) sees that which one who is absent does not
[1:30] If the nobleman of a people comes to you, honour him
[1:31] False oaths lay waste to home (lands)
[1:32] Whoever is killed attempting to save his property is a martyr
[1:33] Actions are through intentions
[1:34] The leader of a people is their servant
[1:35] The best of affairs is that which is balanced
[1:36] O Allah, bless my nation in its early rising on Thursdays
[1:37] Poverty can almost turn into disbelief
[1:38] Travelling is a portion of punishment
[1:39] The best of provision is God-Consciousness (taqwa)
[1:40] Meetings are under trust
