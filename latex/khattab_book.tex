\chapter*{About This Translation}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About This Translation}

\textit{The Clear Quran: An Optimal Translation} is an English translation of
the meanings of the Holy Quran by Dr.~Mustafa Khattab, a Canadian-Egyptian
Muslim scholar who studied at Al-Azhar University in Egypt, one of the oldest
institutions of Islamic learning in the world.

\medskip
This translation aims to convey the meanings of the Quran in clear, contemporary
English that is accessible to the modern reader, without compromising accuracy.
Key features include:

\medskip
\begin{itemize}
  \setlength\itemsep{2pt}
  \item Clear, modern English that preserves the dignity and beauty of the
        original Arabic text.
  \item The use of gender-inclusive language where the original intends both
        men and women.
  \item Footnotes and bracketed additions [\,\textsuperscript{text}\,] clarifying
        implied meanings, represented here as \textsuperscript{raised} markers
        or in-line context notes.
  \item The ˹\,˺ corner brackets are used throughout to enclose implied
        meanings that are in the original Arabic but need elaboration in
        English for clarity.
\end{itemize}

\medskip
This document contains the English translation text only. For Arabic script,
Unicode transliteration, or multi-language comparison, see the full
\texttt{druvx13/Quran-data} repository.
